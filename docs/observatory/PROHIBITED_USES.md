# OSG prohibited-use and public-release terms

OSG public artifacts are provided for research on institutions and aggregate scientific processes. They must not be used to:

- infer, reveal, or validate the identity of an anonymous or pseudonymous reviewer;
- join profiles or external records for the purpose of revealing anonymous participants;
- rank individuals by productivity, quality, employability, fundability, or reviewer performance;
- support employment, promotion, funding, surveillance, harassment, or automated targeting decisions;
- reconstruct, redistribute, or train on source text whose licence does not permit that use;
- circumvent access controls, provider rate limits, deletions, or takedowns.

Public release schemas exclude protected person/reviewer identifiers, signatures, reader lists, and raw/local pointers. Workload and small-cell identity outputs are aggregate-only. Public fixtures and rebuild recipes require no secret or paid API. A source pointer indicates provenance, not permission beyond the source's terms and licence.

These conditions supplement the repository licence for OSG data artifacts. If a source licence is stricter, that source licence controls. Violations or disclosure risks should be reported through the repository security/contact channel so the affected artifact can be quarantined and amended.
